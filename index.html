No. 02 — The Salon

A small room on the New Bedford Road, where the kettle is on and the music is low.

Divine is a hair, nail and beauty room on the New Bedford Road in Luton — a few minutes' walk from the station, opposite the run of shops on the way up to Round Green.

We're a small team: hairdressers, nail technicians and lash artists who took on this space because we wanted somewhere unhurried to work. The chairs are full most Saturdays. Tuesday mornings are gentler — bring a book.

Most of our clients found us by walking past. We'd be glad if you did the same.
